<objective>
Implement the Helpfulness Overhaul depth work for Phase 11's two engines and their frontend surfaces. This plan has three functional concerns:

1. **Lineup Strength 2.0 (LS-01–06)**: Extend `LineupEngine` to benchmark each slot against both the league median starter and the contender-team median starter (top-third by ceiling_score), use statistical median (not average) as the benchmark to resist outlier bias, apply an elite insulation guard that prevents labeling a high-insulation starter as below-replacement from raw recent production alone, compute a per-slot upgrade_leverage_score with an estimated title equity improvement, and separate "weak by median" from "weak relative to contender path" as distinct signal fields. TE premium format adjustments lower urgency weighting for TE weakness in non-TE-premium formats.

2. **Roster Hygiene 2.0 (HYG-01–05)**: Expand `HygieneEngine` from 4 action buckets (consolidate/cut/stash/taxi) to 8 (cut, hold, shop, package, taxi, handcuff_speculative, reroll_into_pick, throw_in_now), attach `timing_rationale` and `packaging_rationale` fields to every suggestion, enforce the productive-veteran protection rule (never classify a player as a sell without a willing-buyer archetype), and guarantee every roster surfaces at least one dead_spot / liquid_shop / package_candidate where evidence exists. Add `player_context_flags` (QB upgrade/downgrade, coaching change, depth-chart competition, injury recovery, age cliff proximity, role compression/expansion) that alter both lineup and hygiene recommendations (REC-06).

3. **Frontend surfaces**: Update `LineupStrengthCard` to show the contender benchmark comparison, an upgrade leverage callout, and elite insulation badges. Create `RosterHygienePanel` (currently missing from the frontend/src/components/lineup/ directory) that renders the 8 action types, timing rationale, packaging rationale, and context flags.

Purpose: The engines as implemented in 11-01 through 11-04 use average benchmarks (vulnerable to outliers), emit only 4 hygiene buckets (missing critical dynasty actions like "shop", "package", "throw-in-now"), have no elite player protection logic, and do not surface player context flags that would alter recommendation text. This plan fills all of those gaps under the Helpfulness Overhaul PRD.
</objective>

    def _contender_pool_ids(

        all_inputs: dict[int, ScorecardInputs],
    ) -> set[int]:
        """Return roster_ids in the top CONTENDER_TIER_FRACTION by ceiling_score."""
        if not scorecards:
